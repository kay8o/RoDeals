How the 40% method works

When someone buys an eligible Marketplace avatar item inside a Roblox game, Roblox pays the owner of that game a commission of up to 40% of the Robux spent. That's the "40% method". The item's creator still earns their own share.

That's why the link above should be a game you own: the commission goes to whoever owns the game where the purchase happens. Eligibility depends on the item and the game, and Roblox sets (and can change) the rates. See Roblox's creator docs for current terms.

Not supported: the Headless Horseman bundle and Limited items made by Roblox can't be used with the 40% method. RoDeals warns you on those pages. UGC Limiteds are supported.

If you haven't added your own game, RoDeals uses the RoDeals creator's game, so any commission from purchases made there goes to them.
